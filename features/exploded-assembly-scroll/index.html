Sheet 01 / Process

We draw it apart, then we put it together.

Every pump skid starts as a stack of loose parts on a drawing. Scroll to watch one close up into a single, bolted assembly.

  1. 01

    Foundation

    A ground base plate and a cast housing set the datum for everything above.

  2. 02

    Rotating group

    Impeller and shaft are balanced together before they go anywhere near the casing.

  3. 03

    Sealing cover

    The cover clamps the seal faces and carries the bearing for the drive end.

  4. 04

    Drive unit

    The motor drops on last, aligned by dowels, and the drawing is complete.
