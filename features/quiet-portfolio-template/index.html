Notes

Why I sketch in greyscale first

Colour hides weak hierarchy. If the layout reads in grey, the colour only has to add mood, not rescue structure.

A small checklist for empty states

Say what is missing, say why, offer one next step. Anything more and people stop reading.

On saying no to features

Every addition has a maintenance cost that nobody budgets. I ask what we would remove to make room.
