Open departures

    Questions walkers ask

    How fit do I need to be?

    If you can hike six hours with a light pack and enjoy stairs, you will be fine. We climb slowly and rest often.

    Where do we sleep?

    Shared bunk rooms of four to eight, with blankets provided. Bring a thin sleeping liner.

    What if the weather turns?

    Your guide picks a lower variant of the day. Every departure keeps a fixed bad-weather route.
