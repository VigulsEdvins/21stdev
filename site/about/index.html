About

A good page for a good small business.

edvlabs makes simple, good-looking web pages for small businesses. You answer a few short questions, then either build the page yourself from ready-made blocks, or we build it for you.

We work from Latvia. You own what you get: the files are yours, and you can put them anywhere.

We keep it plain. No jargon, no lock-in, and a price you can see before you decide.
