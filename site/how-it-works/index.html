How it works

From a few answers to a page you own.

Every way starts with the same short questions. After that you choose who makes the page.

You build it

  1. Answer the questions

    About ten short ones. “I don’t know yet” is always allowed.

  2. Pick blocks and write your words

    The builder opens with the blocks we suggest. Change them, put them in your order, replace the sample words.

  3. Choose the look

    Colours and type, with a preview that updates as you go.

  4. Pay and take your files

    You pay once and download the finished page.

We build it

  1. Answer the questions

    The same ones. We read every answer.

  2. Leave an email address or a phone number

    One is enough. We get in touch.

  3. We agree the price and what you send us

    The price is agreed with you before any work starts.

  4. You get the same finished files

    Ready to put online, and yours to keep.

What you get

  • The finished page as files you own
  • A plain guide to putting it online
  • The editor, if you chose it, to change the words later

What is not included

  • Hosting, the place where the page lives online
  • A domain name, the address of the page
  • Writing your words or taking photos, unless we agree that first
