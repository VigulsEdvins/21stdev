03

Space and shape

Space runs on a 4 pixel base: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96. Corners are 14, 22, 28 and pill. The circle and the arch are the recurring shapes; they are for decoration and images, never for cropping words away.
