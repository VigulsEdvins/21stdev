Good to know

Short answers.

What if I don’t know what I need?

Say so. Every question has an “I don’t know yet” answer, and we suggest a sensible page from the rest.

Can I change the words later?

Yes. Add the editor and you can change the words and pictures yourself whenever you like. Without it, you can still change the files by hand or ask us.

Do I need hosting?

Yes, a page needs somewhere to live online. Hosting and a domain name are not included, and the guide that comes with your files shows how to set both up.

Who owns the page?

You do. You get the files and can put them anywhere.

Can you do it all for me?

Yes. Choose “We build it”, leave an email address or a phone number, and we get in touch.
