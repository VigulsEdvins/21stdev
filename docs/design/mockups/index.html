Two editorial directions for edvlabs

The same three screens in two looks: the home page, question 4 of the questionnaire (“Which parts should your page have?”), and step 2 of the builder (“Words”). All text and prices are samples; the screenshots are real pages from the vault. Click a picture to open the live mock-up.

A — Paper and ink

Calm and warm. A soft serif for headlines, plain sans for the interface, one clay-red accent, thin rules, offset “paper” shadows, numbered sections and a hand-drawn underline.

Home Question 4 of 10 Builder, step 2: Words (steps along the top)
  • Fonts: Young Serif and Public Sans (open licence, self-hosted files)
  • Feels like: an independent print studio
  • Risk: can slide toward “cosy craft”; the round stamp needs a real meaning or should go

B — Newsprint grid

Confident and loud. Heavy black rules, a very large wordmark, tight heavy headlines, italic serif for the human voice, mono labels, a signal red, and prices set out like a ledger.

Home Question 4 of 10 (live receipt on the right) Builder, step 2: Words (steps down the left)
  • Fonts: Libre Franklin, Libre Caslon Text and IBM Plex Mono (open licence, self-hosted files)
  • Feels like: a newspaper front page that explains its prices
  • Risk: loud; “Vol. 1 No. 1” is a gimmick that needs to earn its place
